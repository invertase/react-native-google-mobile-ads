The AdMob module allows you to display adverts to your users. Adverts are served through Google AdMob or
Google Ad Manager, so a [Google AdMob](https://apps.admob.com) or
[Google Ad Manager](https://admanager.google.com) account is required.

The module supports six ad formats:

1. Full screen [App open ads](/ad-formats/app-open).
2. Component based [Banner ads](/ad-formats/banner).
3. Full screen [Interstitial ads](/ad-formats/interstitial).
4. Full screen [Rewarded ads](/ad-formats/rewarded).
5. Full screen [Rewarded interstitial ads](/ad-formats/rewarded-interstitial).
6. Component based [Native ads](/ad-formats/native).
